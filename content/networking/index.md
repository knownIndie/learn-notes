How a web page gets from a name to a screen, and the browser rules that decide what a page may read. Short on time? Start with the quick revision page. Otherwise read the quick pages in order; each one links to its detailed version.

2. **[[networking/http-message|HTTP message]]**: what a request and a response look like, methods and status codes. ([[networking/deep/http-message|detailed]])
3. **[[networking/request-lifecycle|Request lifecycle]]**: what happens when you type a URL and press Enter. ([[networking/deep/request-lifecycle|detailed]])
4. **[[networking/dns|DNS]]**: turning `shop.com` into an IP address. ([[networking/deep/dns|detailed]])
5. **[[networking/tcp|TCP and UDP]]**: the reliable connection, the three-way handshake, and when UDP is better. ([[networking/deep/tcp|detailed]])
6. **[[networking/tls|TLS and HTTPS]]**: encryption, certificates, and the handshake. ([[networking/deep/tls|detailed]])
7. **[[networking/http-caching|HTTP caching]]**: `Cache-Control`, ETags and 304. ([[networking/deep/http-caching|detailed]])
8. **[[networking/origin|Origin and same-origin policy]]**: scheme + host + port, and what the browser blocks. ([[networking/deep/origin|detailed]])
9. **[[networking/cors|CORS]]**: how a server lets other origins read its replies. ([[networking/deep/cors|detailed]])
